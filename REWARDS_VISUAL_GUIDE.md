## ✨ User Experience Flow

1. **Discover**: See "How It Works" tab explaining system
2. **Earn**: Perform actions, see popup "+X points earned!"
3. **Track**: Watch progress bar fill in profile
4. **Unlock**: Automatically earn badges at milestones
5. **Redeem**: Browse rewards and exchange points
6. **Celebrate**: Receive confirmation and enjoy reward
